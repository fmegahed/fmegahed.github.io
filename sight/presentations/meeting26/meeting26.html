Development Time: Original Modules and the Makino Builds

What changed

  • The first three machines took four to six months each to reach a working module.
  • With our AI-based pipeline, the Makino D200Z was produced overnight and the Makino UX3i in about two days, including revisions.
  • This shows that new machines can be added in hours to days, not months.

Scope

  • Original modules: dates of the first working module reported in the meeting minutes, measured from the Sep 26, 2025 start of the hazard outlines (Meetings 6, 10, 13, and 17). That work included a vendor team (MeetKai), 3D assets built from scratch, and the design of the training structure itself.
  • Makino builds: elapsed working time reported by the team. Both are self-initiated demonstrations; Makino has been contacted for feedback.
