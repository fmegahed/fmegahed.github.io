What Makes It Work
  • 100 Hz real-time data acquisition (10 ms cadence)
  • Computer-vision safety zones with dynamic margins
  • Hand-gesture pause / resume commands

A Problem DOE Wants Solved — and So Does YMMC

  • Phase I of DOE Genesis demands quantitative proof that physics-informed AI + digital twins move the needle on discovery, prediction, and control.
  • Genesis Challenge Area 1D maps directly to YMMC:
    • Physics-informed AI methods and digital twins for industrial processes.
    • Advanced controls for energy-intensive, cross-sector production — e.g., coatings.
  • DOE strongly encourages industrial partners on proposal teams — matching the baseline-energy prediction questions you and Belize raised with us.
